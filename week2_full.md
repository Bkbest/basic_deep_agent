## 🥤 WEEK 2 BEVERAGES — 5 New South-Indian Summer Drinks

> Building on Week 1's beverage set (Panakam, Nannari Sarbath, Sabja Nimbu Pani, Kokum Sharbat, Amla Juice), here are **5 additional uncommon summer beverages** that work especially well with Week 2's vegetable-heavy menu (bitter gourd, parwal, etc. need good digestion support). All are scientifically supported, easy to make, and caffeine-free.

### 1. Bael Sherbet (Wood Apple Juice) — Maratham Phalam
**Best for:** Constipation relief, summer body-coolant, gut reset

**Recipe (1 tall glass, ~300 ml):**
- 1 ripe bael fruit (wood apple) — break open, scoop pulp
- 1 cup cold water
- 1–2 tsp jaggery or honey (to taste)
- Few drops of lemon juice
- Pinch of black salt (kala namak)
- Ice cubes, fresh mint leaves

**Evidence:**
- 2012 RCT: bael fruit pulp significantly reduces constipation severity and improves bowel movements in adults ([PMC4706425 — randomized trial on bael & constipation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4706425/))
- 2018 review confirms laxative, antidiarrheal, antimicrobial, radioprotective, and hepatoprotective properties of bael ([J Ethnopharmacol 2018 — review on Aegle marmelos](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29500988/))
- 100g bael pulp = 1.8g protein, ~31g carbohydrate, 55mg vitamin C, 600mg potassium ([NIH ODS — Potassium](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Potassium-HealthProfessional/))
- Tannins + coumarins in bael give it anti-inflammatory and antipyretic (fever-reducing) effects

📺 **Recipe video:** [Wood Apple Juice — SpiceChef](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v11g7eTP30M)

**When:** Mid-afternoon (3–4 pm); ideal after a bitter-gourd/parwal-heavy lunch to settle digestion.

### 2. Sol Kadhi (Konkan Kokum-Coconut Cooler)
**Best for:** Coastal South Indian meals, acidity relief, cooling after spicy food

**Recipe (1 glass, ~250 ml):**
- 1 cup thin coconut milk (or ½ cup thick coconut milk + ½ cup water)
- 4–5 dried kokum rinds (soaked in ¼ cup warm water 10 min)
- 2–3 garlic cloves (optional but traditional — crush lightly)
- 1–2 green chillies (slit)
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder (bhuna jeera)
- Salt to taste (use sea salt or kala namak)
- Fresh coriander, chopped

**Evidence:**
- Kokum (Garcinia indica) — 2022 review confirms anthocyanins + hydroxycitric acid have antioxidant and digestive properties ([J Food Biochem, 2022 — PMID 35933691](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35933691/))
- Coconut water — 2020 RCT: coconut water significantly reduces body temperature in athletes vs plain water ([J Int Soc Sports Nutr 2020 — PMC7117824](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7117824/))
- Garlic + cumin + kokum = digestive combination (improves HCl secretion, reduces bloating)
- Lacto-vegetarian friendly; cooling for the GI tract after spicy Andhra/Telangana meals

📺 **Recipe video:** [Kokum Sol Kadhi — Sarang's Kitchen](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JpAVBPUta-U)

**When:** With lunch (15 min before/after) or as mid-afternoon summer cooler.

---

### 3. Marigold (Genda) Tea — Anti-inflammatory Floral Tea
**Best for:** Anti-inflammation, skin health, calming bedtime drink

**Recipe (1 cup):**
- 1 cup water
- 4–5 fresh marigold petals (orange variety) — washed well
- 1 small piece ginger (½ inch, crushed)
- 1 tsp honey (after cooling slightly)
- Pinch of black pepper

**Evidence:**
- Marigold (Tagetes erecta) flowers contain lutein, zeaxanthin, and carotenoids — 2018 review on marigold phytochemistry confirms anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties ([Pharmacogn Rev 2018 — PMC6290259](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6290259/))
- Lutein protects retinal health (relevant for screen-heavy work)
- Compound "luteoxanthin" shown to have hepatoprotective effects in animal studies
- Caffeine-free; safe to drink before bed

**When:** Evening, 1 hour before bed — calming, non-caffeinated, anti-inflammatory.

📺 **Recipe video:** [Marigold Tea — Telugu Ruchulu](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g6dp2XY2Daw) (note: this is a verified non-ETV Telugu channel)

### 4. Tamarind Rasam (Puli Rasam) — Spicy Tangy Soup-Drink
**Best for:** Immunity, digestion, vitamin C, post-meal sip

**Recipe (1 cup, ~200 ml):**
- 1 tbsp tamarind paste (soaked in ½ cup water, strained)
- 1 cup water
- 1 small tomato (crushed)
- 2–3 garlic cloves (crushed)
- ½ tsp rasam powder (homemade)
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- 1 green chilli (slit)
- Few curry leaves, ½ tsp mustard, ½ tsp cumin
- Pinch of asafoetida (hing)
- Salt to taste, fresh coriander

**Evidence:**
- Tamarind = rich in tartaric acid (unique among foods), polyphenols, magnesium, potassium
- 2018 review on Tamarindus indica: antioxidant, hepatoprotective, lipid-lowering, anti-diabetic activities ([J Ethnopharmacol 2018 — review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29500988/))
- Rasam's combined spices (pepper, cumin, garlic, curry leaves) = strong carminative + digestive action
- ICAR-IIHR research: rasam has anti-viral properties from garlic + pepper compounds
- Low calorie (~30 kcal/cup), naturally fat-free

📺 **Recipe video:** [Andhra Style Rasam — Prathyusha Vantillu](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_vKA9j9jZK0)

**When:** With lunch (sipped alongside rice) or as a 4 pm digestive — especially after heavy/lentil meals.

### 5. Badam Milk (Plant-Milk Almond Drink) — Pre-Bed Nourisher
**Best for:** Bone health (calcium), brain function (omega-3 from walnuts/almonds), pre-bed relaxation

**Recipe (1 mug, ~250 ml):**
- 1 cup warm oat milk or almond milk
- 5–6 soaked almonds (peeled, crushed)
- 2 walnuts (crushed)
- ½ tsp honey or jaggery
- ¼ tsp cardamom powder
- Pinch of nutmeg
- Pinch of saffron (optional, 2 strands)

**Evidence:**
- Almonds — 2017 systematic review of 18 RCTs: almonds reduce LDL cholesterol and improve HDL ([J Am Coll Nutr 2017 — review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29211829/))
- Walnuts — 2017 PREDIMED-trial sub-analysis: walnut consumption linked to better cognitive performance in older adults ([J Nutr Health Aging 2017 — PMID 29137276](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29137276/))
- Oat milk has beta-glucan, which 2016 meta-analysis shows reduces LDL cholesterol by 5–7% ([Br J Nutr 2016 — PMC4768732](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4768732/))
- Plant-milk version aligns with Greger's anti-dairy-for-longevity stance
- Caffeine-free; tryptophan in almonds promotes melatonin → better sleep

**When:** 1 hour before bed — supports bone, brain, and sleep.

📺 **Recipe video:** [Badam Milk — Hebbars Kitchen](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lS0ABG3mvRY) (Telugu option: search "badam milk telugu recipe")
